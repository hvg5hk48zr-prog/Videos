# Brazil's Real Heroes Were Fighting for Freedom

A 60-second vertical (1080×1920, 30 fps) Instagram Reel about **Chico Rei** and
**Dragão do Mar**, two symbols of resistance to slavery in Brazil.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `out/brazils_real_heroes_reel.mp4` | Final reel with narration (H.264 + AAC, −14 LUFS) |
| `out/brazils_real_heroes_reel_no_voiceover.mp4` | Same picture and sound design without narration, so you can record your own voice in the Instagram editor |
| `out/cover.jpg` | 9:16 cover with the title for the Reels grid |
| `out/cover_realistic.jpg` / `out/capa_realista_pt.jpg` | Higher-fidelity painted covers (English / Portuguese) |
| `out/herois_do_brasil_40s_pt.mp4` | **40-second Portuguese cut**: hook in the first frame, Portuguese text, subtitles and narration |
| `out/herois_do_brasil_40s_pt_sem_narracao.mp4` | Same 40 s cut without narration, for recording your own voice |

Everything (picture, music, sound effects) is generated by the code in this folder.
There's no stock footage, so there are no licensing issues. The visuals are a
stylised, silhouette-based **artistic reconstruction**, and a small label on
screen says so throughout. That keeps the fictional scenes from being mistaken
for historical footage and avoids depicting the people's faces or bodies in a
stereotyped way.

The hero shots of Chico Rei (10.8–15.2 s) and Dragão do Mar (33–38 s), and the final
close-up, use the photographic portraits in `assets/`, with a slow push-in and dust
drifting through the light. These are illustrative portraits, not likenesses: no
portrait of Chico Rei exists, and the image is not the real Francisco José do
Nascimento. That's why the "artistic reconstruction" label stays on screen.

## Structure

| Time | Scene | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 s | **The question.** Black, heartbeat, chains. Flashes of a colonial hill town, a gold mine, a slave ship in a storm, shackled wrists | WHO ARE BRAZIL'S REAL HEROES? |
| 5–20 s | **Chico Rei.** Miners in torchlight in Vila Rica (Minas Gerais), a dignified man lifting his head toward a shaft of light, gold dust rising from cupped hands, an iron cuff opening and falling away | CHICO REI · FREEDOM, DIGNITY, RESISTANCE. |
| 20–38 s | **Dragão do Mar.** Fortaleza, Ceará, 1881: storm waves against jangadas, a jangadeiro facing the slave ship offshore, the others joining him, the line of men who will not sail | DRAGÃO DO MAR · THE MAN WHO SAID NO. |
| 38–50 s | **The legacy.** Fast cuts: dawn ocean, hands breaking chains, faces looking into the camera, people standing together, a jangada sailing past modern Fortaleza | narration + subtitles |
| 50–60 s | **The final question.** Silence, a close-up, black | WHO DO WE CALL A HERO? · REMEMBER THOSE WHO FOUGHT FOR FREEDOM. |

**Narration (38–50 s):**
> One represents the fight to reclaim freedom. The other helped turn refusal
> into collective resistance. They remind us that history was also made by
> those who fought against oppression.

Subtitles are burned in, because most Reels are watched with the sound off. All
text stays inside Instagram's safe zone, clear of the top bar and the caption and
buttons at the bottom.

## Historical notes

* **Chico Rei.** His story comes mostly from **oral tradition** in Ouro Preto
  (formerly Vila Rica), first written down in the early 20th century. Historians
  debate how much of it is documented fact. The reel presents him as a symbol
  ("oral tradition says…") rather than claiming documentary certainty.
* **Dragão do Mar.** Francisco José do Nascimento (1839–1914). In January and
  August 1881 the jangadeiros of Fortaleza, organised with abolitionists including
  José Luís Napoleão and the Sociedade Cearense Libertadora, refused to ferry
  enslaved people out to the ships that would sell them to the south. Their slogan
  was *"No porto do Ceará não se embarcam mais escravos."* Ceará declared
  abolition on 25 March 1884. Brazil as a whole followed in 1888 with the Lei Áurea.

## Rebuilding

Requirements: Python 3 with `numpy` and `Pillow`, `ffmpeg`, and Piper TTS for the
narration (`pip install piper-tts`, plus the `en-us-ryan-high` voice from the
[Piper v0.0.2 release](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/releases/tag/v0.0.2)).

```sh
export PIPER_VOICE=/path/to/en-us-ryan-high.onnx
python3 build.py          # narration, soundtrack, ~1800 frames, final MP4s and cover
python3 preview.py sheet.png 3.5,13,35,57   # contact sheet of chosen timestamps
PIPER_VOICE_PT=/path/to/pt-br-edresson-low.onnx python3 cut_pt.py   # 40 s Portuguese cut
python3 cover_realistic.py pt                                        # Portuguese cover
```

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `timeline.py` | Edit list: shots, camera moves, colour grades, on-screen text, narration timing |
| `scenes.py` | One renderer per shot (mine, sea, faces, chains…) |
| `gfx.py` | Noise textures, silhouettes, jangadas, ships, chains, lighting helpers |
| `render.py` | Camera, grade, film grain, vignette, flicker, text compositing |
| `audio.py` | Synthesised score and sound design, timed from the same constants as the picture |
| `build.py` | Orchestration, loudness normalisation, muxing and covers |
| `assets/` | Photographic portraits used for the Chico Rei and Dragão do Mar hero shots and the final close-up |

To change wording, timing or the narration text, edit `timeline.py` and run
`python3 build.py` again.
